How the numbers are worked out
  • NIPUN % of students: NIPUN students divided by students assessed, Grades 1 and 2 together. Grade cards use that grade alone.
  • A school is NIPUN when both Grade 1 and Grade 2 have 80% or more of assessed students at NIPUN level. This matches the status in both source sheets.
  • "One grade not assessed" means one of the two grades had no students assessed in that round, so no school status is given. "Not assessed" means the school is not in that round's results sheet.
  • "Incomplete" in Movement says which round lacks a school status: endline, baseline, or both.
  • The "How close schools are to NIPUN" chart places each school by the lower of its Grade 1 and Grade 2 NIPUN %.
  • Schools are matched across rounds by UDISE code. Block and ARP come from the endline ARP list. Some schools appear in only one round.
  • Change is endline minus baseline, in percentage points (pp).
